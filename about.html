About Fashion By Nilu

Crafting excellence through passion and precision

My Story

Fashion By Nilu was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to make premium custom tailoring accessible to everyone. With over 10 years of experience in the fashion and tailoring industry, I bring expertise, passion and dedication to every project.

What started as a passion for tailoring has grown into a thriving solo operation. As a one-person business, I personally handle every aspect—from consultation and design to measurements and stitching. This hands-on approach ensures that every garment meets my high standards of quality.

I believe that great fashion is not just about following trends—it's about creating pieces that make you feel confident and beautiful. Every garment I create is a reflection of my dedication to excellence and my respect for the craft of tailoring.

Our Values

Quality

We use premium fabrics and employ traditional tailoring techniques combined with modern precision to ensure exceptional quality in every piece.

Trust

Building long-term relationships with our customers through transparency, reliability and consistent delivery of our promises.

Passion

We are passionate about fashion and tailoring. This passion drives us to innovate and create designs that exceed expectations.

Innovation

While honoring traditional tailoring craftsmanship, we embrace modern techniques and trends to create contemporary, stylish pieces.
